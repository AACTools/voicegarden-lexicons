voxcommunis-only

merged.tsv is the build file and the bundle's single source (an identical
copy of voxcommunis.folded.tsv, kept under the conventional name so the
ByT5 corpus builder, which reads staging/<lang>/merged.tsv, sees Tswana).
CV20-validated vocabulary filter (min 2 alignments) + fold_tswana.py
convention fold (aspiration stripped, i-merger, zh->sh). Pronunciations
are dictionary-driven (the cvu aligner's G2P output); the audio gates
which words ship. No seed dictionary exists for Tswana.
